PTB-XL MI CLASSIFICATION - PRELIMINARY RESULTS
============================================================

Dataset subset:
Train: 800 ECGs
Validation: 200 ECGs
Test: 200 ECGs

         Model  Accuracy  Precision  Recall  F1_Score  ROC_AUC
CNN (ResNet18)      76.0      51.56    66.0     57.89    80.75
   Transformer      73.5      48.35    88.0     62.41    84.46
        Hybrid      78.5      55.93    66.0     60.55    84.81

Observations:
- Hybrid produced the highest overall accuracy.
- Hybrid produced the highest MI precision.
- Transformer produced the highest MI recall/sensitivity.
- Transformer produced the highest MI F1 score.
- Hybrid produced the highest ROC-AUC.
- Results are preliminary and were obtained from a stratified PTB-XL subset rather than the entire dataset.
